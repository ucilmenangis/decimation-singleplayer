# Apartment interiors: references and review checklist

Round 2 step 3 (7 Oktober 2026). Collected once; read this instead of
searching again. Scale: 1 block ~ 1 m; storeys have 3 blocks of air.

## Real world rules (interior design guides)

- Main walkway 60 to 90 cm, comfortably ~90 cm -> 1 block free path from
  the entry door to every room door. Secondary paths 45 to 60 cm ->
  still 1 block in Minecraft (nothing narrower exists).
- Sofa to coffee table 40 to 45 cm; coffee table at most 2/3 of the sofa
  length -> table directly in front of the sofa (0 to 1 block gap), 1 to 2
  blocks long for a 3 block sofa.
- Leave a little room behind a sofa (8 to 13 cm): a sofa may back onto a
  wall, or float in a larger room facing the TV.
- Bed: 60 to 90 cm free along the sides people use; at least ~60 cm
  between bed side and wall on the used side; ~90 cm between bed and a
  swinging door -> one free block along at least one long side, the
  door never opens onto the bed.
- Kitchen work triangle (sink, stove, fridge): each leg 1.2 to 2.7 m, sum
  under 8 m -> the three within a 4 to 6 block run or an L; walkway in
  front of counters ~90 cm to 1 m -> 1 free row in front of the run;
  kitchen entrance at least 70 to 80 cm -> 1 block.
Sources: Homes & Gardens living room clearances
(homesandgardens.com/interior-design/living-rooms/a-guide-to-living-room-clearances-measurements-and-spacing),
Arcedior coffee table guide (arcedior.com/blog/coffee-table-size-guide),
Dimensions.com queen / king bedroom layouts, JLC kitchen clearances and
field guide (jlconline.com), Kitchen Cabinet Kings work triangle glossary.

## Minecraft furniture techniques (builder practice, 1.7.10 subset)

Commonly used by builders (seen across interior idea guides); only the
ones possible in 1.7.10 are listed (no banners, sea lanterns, iron
trapdoors, coloured fences, item frames as blocks):
- Sofa: stairs in a row, trapdoors on the open ends as armrests (oak
  trapdoor only in 1.7.10); quartz stairs = modern white, dark wood =
  rustic. Armchair: one stair with trapdoors on both sides.
- Coffee table: a slab on the floor, or a pressure plate on a fence post;
  a carpet rug under the group.
- Lighting: glowstone under a carpet (soft floor glow), a lamp of fence +
  glowstone / torch; ceiling lights (Decimation BlockLight panels here).
- Kitchen: counters of slabs / quartz with cabinets; sink = cauldron with
  a tripwire hook tap; fridge = iron blocks (here Decimation
  ElectricBoxBin); upper cabinets on the wall.
- Bathroom: toilet = quartz stairs or a hopper with a trapdoor lid; sink =
  cauldron + tripwire hook; tub = cauldrons or a slab basin; tiles.
- Walls: bookshelves, paintings (entities, can be spawned by world
  generation), curtains of white / grey wool beside windows.
- Colour: neutral base (white, grey, wood) with one or two accent colours
  (rug, sofa, cushions); avoid many saturated colours.

## Apartment checklist (what a reviewer checks per room)

Whole flat:
1. A free 1 block path from the entry door to every room door; no
   furniture on or directly in front of a door.
2. Every room reads as its function from the doorway within 2 seconds
   (focal object visible: TV + sofa, bed, kitchen run, toilet).
3. 40 to 60% of the floor free; furniture grouped, not scattered.
4. Neutral palette with 1 or 2 accents; floor, walls, ceiling distinct.
5. Decay is plausible (some items missing, debris low, not random cubes).
Living room: sofa faces the TV; TV on a cabinet or the wall, not on the
floor; coffee table and rug between them; a lamp or plant; seating not
blocking the walkway.
Kitchen: fridge, sink, stove in one run or L with counters between;
upper cabinets above counters (not above the fridge gap if it would
collide); a free row in front; tiles or stone floor; dining table near.
Bedroom: bed head against a wall, at least one long side free;
nightstand at the head; wardrobe or dresser; not blocking the window
with tall pieces.
Bathroom: toilet, basin, bath / shower in a tiled room with a door;
nothing blocking the door swing.
Lobby / corridors: clear, lit, mailboxes or bikes / plants, stair access
obvious.
